This is where NeoGauge began

Modern information.
Even on an analogue bike.

Many classic and modern-retro motorcycles — including the Kawasaki Z650RS that inspired the idea — keep the charm of a needle speedometer while showing very little on their small LCD display.

NeoGauge turns the iPhone into a complementary digital instrument: readable in landscape, designed to stay visible while you use it and focused on the information you need at a glance.

It does not replace the original instruments and it is not a navigation app.

A precise choice

It does not tell you where to go.
It tells you about your bike.

NeoGauge is not a navigation app. It does not provide directions, plan itineraries, show maps or create a route file.

It keeps useful session summaries — such as distance, duration and speed — together with the notes and photos you choose to add.

In brief

Frequently asked questions.

Is NeoGauge a navigation app?+

No. NeoGauge provides no directions, itinerary planning or map-based route display.

Can I use it on iPhone and iPad?+

Yes. NeoGauge uses the same data model with an interface adapted to both devices.

Is NeoGauge free?+

Yes. The first motorcycle, Dashboard and main functions are free with advertising. NeoGauge Plus and the second motorcycle are optional, separate in-app purchases.

What does NeoGauge Plus include?+

Plus removes advertising, enables the photo diary and shareable ride cards, saves refuelling GPS positions and unlocks lean-angle maximum history and management.

What happens when Plus expires?+

Notes, photos and saved refuelling positions remain available in read-only mode. Lean-angle history and management require an active Plus subscription.

Where can I download NeoGauge?+

NeoGauge is free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad. Open the official App Store page.
